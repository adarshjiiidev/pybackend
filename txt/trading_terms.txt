# Basic Trading Terms & Definitions

## LTP (Last Traded Price)

**Definition:** LTP stands for "Last Traded Price" - the most recent price at which a stock, commodity, or derivative contract was traded on the exchange.

**Key Points:**
- LTP is the current market price you see on trading platforms
- It represents the last successful transaction between a buyer and seller
- Updates in real-time during market hours (NSE: 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM)
- Different from:
  - **Bid Price:** Highest price buyers are willing to pay
  - **Ask Price:** Lowest price sellers are willing to accept
  - **Close Price:** Final trading price of the previous session
  - **Open Price:** First trade price of the current session

**Real-World Example:**
```
Reliance Industries:
- LTP: ₹2,450.75
- Bid: ₹2,450.00 (buyers want to buy at this price)
- Ask: ₹2,451.00 (sellers want to sell at this price)
- Last trade happened at ₹2,450.75
```

**Trading Context:**
When you place a **Market Order**, your order will execute near the current LTP. However, in volatile markets, the actual execution price may differ slightly from the LTP you saw when placing the order.

**Why LTP Matters:**
1. **Price Discovery:** Shows real market consensus at that moment
2. **Decision Making:** Helps decide entry/exit points
3. **Trend Analysis:** LTP movement indicates market sentiment
4. **Premium Calculation:** For options, LTP shows current premium

**Common Questions:**

**Q: What's the difference between LTP and CMP?**  
A: They're the same thing. CMP (Current Market Price) = LTP (Last Traded Price)

**Q: Why does LTP keep changing?**  
A: Every new trade updates the LTP. In liquid stocks, this happens every second.

**Q: Can I buy at LTP?**  
A: Not guaranteed. You can place a limit order at LTP, but if the market moves, you might not get filled. Market orders execute near LTP but price isn't guaranteed.

**Q: What if there's no LTP (0.00)?**  
A: This happens when no trades have occurred yet for the day. Common in illiquid stocks or derivatives contracts.

## Other Common Trading Terms

**CMP (Current Market Price):** Same as LTP - current trading price

**Volume:** Total number of shares/contracts traded

**OI (Open Interest):** Total number of outstanding derivative contracts (Futures/Options)

**Bid-Ask Spread:** Difference between bid and ask price

**Circuit Breaker:** Price limits that halt trading if price moves too drastically
