# Imaginary Line - The Dividing Line

## What is Imaginary Line?

**Definition:** The line that separates IN THE MONEY (ITM) from OUT OF THE MONEY (OTM) options

**Hindi:** Ek aisi line jo IN THE MONEY ko OUT OF THE MONEY se alag karti hai

## Key Concepts

### In The Money (ITM) - Ander Ki Taraf

**Call Side:** Smaller strike prices than imaginary line are ITM  
**Put Side:** Larger strike prices than imaginary line are ITM

**Example:**
If imaginary line is at 17500:
- Calls: 17450, 17400, 17350 are ITM (smaller = ander)
- Puts: 17550, 17600, 17650 are ITM (larger = ander)

### Out Of The Money (OTM) - Bahar Ki Taraf

**Call Side:** Larger strike prices than imaginary line are OTM  
**Put Side:** Smaller strike prices than imaginary line are OTM

**Example:**
If imaginary line is at 17500:
- Calls: 17550, 17600, 17650 are OTM (larger = bahar)
- Puts: 17450, 17400, 17350 are OTM (smaller = bahar)

## Why Imaginary Line Matters

### For Support and Resistance Identification

**Rule 1:** Support/Resistance must be closest to imaginary line  
**Rule 2:** We only consider OI/Volume near the imaginary line  
**Rule 3:** Support is on PUT side, Resistance is on CALL side

**Example:**
```
Current Market: 17485 (Imaginary Line)

Put Side OI:
17500 - 10 Lakh ← Closest to line (15 points away)
17450 - 8 Lakh  ← Further from line
17400 - 12 Lakh ← Too far from line

Support = 17500 ✓ (closest to imaginary line)
```

### For Weakness Detection (WTB/WTT)

**Rule:** Weakness is calculated based on second-highest value near imaginary line

- Can ONLY find WTB/WTT near the imaginary line
- Too far from line = not relevant for current market
- Imaginary line shifts as market moves

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Market at 17500
```
Imaginary Line: 17500

Calls:
17650 (OTM) - Too far, ignore
17600 (OTM) - Relevant
17550 (OTM) - Relevant ← Resistance here (closest)
17500 (ATM) - At the line
17450 (ITM) - Relevant
17400 (ITM) - Relevant ← Support here (closest)
17350 (ITM) - Too far, ignore

Puts:
17550 (ITM) - Relevant ← Resistance same as call
17500 (ATM) - At the line  
17450 (ITM) - Relevant ← Support same as put
```

### Example 2: Call Side Focus
```
If imaginary line is 5000:
- 5000 is CLOSER to 6000
- So call side: smaller (5000) is ITM, larger (6000) is OTM
```

### Example 3: Put Side Focus
```
If imaginary line is between 5000 and 7000:
- 7000 is LARGER number
- So put side: larger (7000) is ITM, smaller (5000) is OTM
```

## Remember

1. Imaginary line MOVES with market
2. Always check both OI and Volume near the line
3. Closest to line = Most relevant
4. ITM = Ander (inside), OTM = Bahar (outside)
5. ITM/OTM definition REVERSES between Call and Put side
