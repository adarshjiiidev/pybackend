# COA 2.0 - Direction from OI Change

## What is COA 2.0?

**Definition:** Predicting market direction using **OI Change** (Open Interest Change).
**Usage:** Used primarily at **Diversion** points (EOR/EOS) to confirm reversal.

## The 6 Basic Rules

### 1. Bearish Signals (Market Down)
- **Call OI Change Increasing (↑)** AND **Put OI Change Decreasing (↓)** = **Blood Bath** (Strong Sell)
- **Call OI Change Increasing (↑)** AND **Put OI Change Stable** = **Bearish**
- **Call OI Change Stable** AND **Put OI Change Decreasing (↓)** = **Bearish**
- **Put OI Change Decreasing (↓)** = Generally Bearish (Support leaving)

### 2. Bullish Signals (Market Up)
- **Call OI Change Decreasing (↓)** AND **Put OI Change Increasing (↑)** = **Bull Run** (Strong Buy)
- **Call OI Change Decreasing (↓)** AND **Put OI Change Stable** = **Bullish**
- **Call OI Change Stable** AND **Put OI Change Increasing (↑)** = **Bullish**
- **Call OI Change Decreasing (↓)** = Generally Bullish (Resistance leaving)

### 3. Range Bound / Unknown Signals
- **Both Stable:** Range Bound (Safety)
- **Both Increasing:** Range Bound (Fighting)
- **Both Decreasing:** Unpredictable (COA 2.0 fails here)

## Detailed Scenarios Table

| Call OI Change | Put OI Change | Market Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Increasing ↑ | Decreasing ↓ | **Blood Bath (Strong Down)** |
| Decreasing ↓ | Increasing ↑ | **Bull Run (Strong Up)** |
| Increasing ↑ | Stable | Bearish |
| Stable | Decreasing ↓ | Bearish |
| Decreasing ↓ | Stable | Bullish |
| Stable | Increasing ↑ | Bullish |
| Stable | Stable | Range Bound (Reversal from EOR/EOS) |
| Increasing ↑ | Increasing ↑ | Range Bound |
| Decreasing ↓ | Decreasing ↓ | **Unpredictable** (Avoid trade) |

## When to use COA 2.0?

1. **At Diversion Points:** When market hits EOR, EOS, EOR+1, or EOS-1.
2. **Confirmation:** Before taking a trade, check COA 2.0.
   - Example: Market at EOR (Resistance). You want to sell PE (Bullish trade? No, Sell CE or Buy Put).
   - *Correction:* At EOR we sell Call (Bearish trade).
   - Check COA 2.0: If Call OI Change is Increasing (Writers adding positions), it CONFIRMS the resistance. Safe to trade.

3. **During SOC:** If market is confused by OI, check trends in OI Change.

## Simple Logic:
- **Call Writers (Call OI Change) = Bears.** If they increase, Market falls.
- **Put Writers (Put OI Change) = Bulls.** If they increase, Market rises.
- **If Writers LEAVE (Decrease) = Opposite side wins.** (Call writers leave = Bulls win).
