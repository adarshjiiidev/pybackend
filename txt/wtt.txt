# WTT (Weak Towards Top) on LTP Calculator

## Definition
WTT (Weak Towards Top) = Support or Resistance is trying to shift towards HIGHER strike prices (top of option chain).
When WTT is present, a YELLOW BOX appears in the Volume or Open Interest column.

IMPORTANT — Option chain is INVERTED:
- Smaller strike prices are displayed at TOP of screen (bottom of the chain)
- Larger strike prices are displayed at BOTTOM of screen (top of the chain)
- "Top" = higher strike prices = lower on screen

---

## What Causes WTT?
The SECOND-HIGHEST value in Volume or OI column is at a HIGHER strike price than the current support/resistance.
This means the market is considering shifting that level UPWARD.

---

## WTT Conditions (based on whether S/R is Volume-only, OI-only, or both)

### If S/R is based solely on Volume OR Open Interest:

CONDITION 1 (Strong):
- The single factor (Volume or OI) at current S/R is strong = support/resistance is also strong/WTT/WTB accordingly.

CONDITION 2 (WTT from other factor):
- If S/R is based on one factor (say OI) and the other factor (Volume) is WTT (second highest at higher strike and approaching current S/R from top/bottom):
  - CALL side: yellow box from other factor = WTB (if moving toward imaginary line from top)
  - PUT side: yellow box from other factor = WTT (if moving toward imaginary line from bottom)
  - If yellow box does NOT cross/bypass the existing S/R → no effect, S/R remains strong
  - If yellow box CROSSES or BYPASSES existing S/R → resistance becomes WTB, support becomes WTT

CONDITION 3 (S/R status changes):
- If the other factor's yellow box crosses existing S/R:
  - Resistance → WTB | Support → WTT
  - S/R STRIKE does NOT change, only the STATUS changes

### If S/R is based on BOTH Volume AND Open Interest:

CONDITION 1:
- Both strong = S/R strong
- Both weak, both trying to move away from imaginary line → S/R weak only if BOTH move away

CONDITION 2:
- Either OI or Volume tries to move CLOSER to imaginary line → S/R is weak
  - Resistance → WTB | Support → WTT

CONDITION 3:
- Both weak but one is WTT and other is WTB → priority to the one moving CLOSER to imaginary line
  - Result: Resistance → WTB | Support → WTT

---

## WTT at Support — What it means:
- Support is trying to shift to a HIGHER strike price
- BULLISH pressure — market may not fall as far as current support
- Market likely to reverse higher (from the new potential support level)
- WTT % INCREASING = support shifting up = market going UP (bullish)
- WTT % DECREASING = support becoming strong = market may fall (bearish)
- WTT DISAPPEARS (% < 75%) = support becomes strong = BEARISH pressure (EOS-1 becomes target)

## WTT at Resistance — What it means:
- Resistance is trying to shift to a HIGHER strike price
- BULLISH pressure — resistance about to break out upward
- WTT % INCREASING = resistance shifting up = market going UP (bullish, COA 1.3 or 1.5)
- WTT % DECREASING = resistance becoming strong = bearish
- WTT DISAPPEARS = resistance becomes strong = BEARISH pressure (EOS-1 becomes market bottom)

---

## Natural Weakness After Shifting (CRITICAL — IGNORE THIS)
After support shifts from 24300 to 24400:
- 24300 automatically shows a yellow box (second highest now)
- This yellow box at 24300 looks like WTB — but it is NOT
- This is NATURAL WEAKNESS from the shift — IGNORE IT
- Only consider 24300 yellow box as real WTB if:
  1. Yellow box at 24300 first disappeared (% dropped below second-highest threshold)
  2. Then yellow box reappeared at 24300 again

---

## Percentage Rules (Game of Percentage — WTT)

### WTT at Support:
- Scenario 1: WTT % INCREASING (e.g., 93.65% → 95% → 99%) → support will shift up → BULLISH
- Scenario 2: WTT % DECREASING (e.g., 93.65% → 90% → 84%) → support becoming strong → BEARISH
- Scenario 3: WTT % drops to 75% and yellow box disappears → support is strong → BEARISH pressure → market bottom = EOS-1

### WTT at Resistance:
- Scenario 1: WTT % INCREASING → resistance will shift up → BULLISH
- Scenario 2: WTT % DECREASING → resistance becoming strong → BEARISH
- Scenario 3: WTT % drops to 75% and disappears → resistance is strong → BEARISH pressure → market bottom = EOS-1
