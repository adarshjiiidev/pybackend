## What is the Yellow Box?
Yellow box = SECOND-HIGHEST value in Volume or OI column.
The Yellow Box is the core indicator for WTB and WTT.
Where the yellow box appears determines if it is WTB or WTT, and at what percentage it is shows how close the shift is.

---

## GAME OF PERCENTAGE — WTB (Weak Towards Bottom)

### WTB at SUPPORT (Example: Support=24400, WTB at 24300 = 92.93%)

SCENARIO 1: WTB % INCREASING (e.g., 92.93% → 95% → 99%)
- Support AT 24400 will shift DOWN to 24300
- Market expected to fall to 24300 (not just reverse at 24400)
- → BEARISH pressure increasing
- After shift: Yellow box appears at 24400 = NATURAL WEAKNESS → IGNORE IT
  (True WTB at 24300 means: market falls further to 24300 level)

SCENARIO 2: WTB % DECREASING (e.g., 92.93% → 90% → 85% → 80%)
- Support at 24400 is becoming STRONGER
- Market will reverse from EOS of 24400 (not fall to 24300)
- → BULLISH pressure building (failed bearish attempt)

SCENARIO 3: WTB % drops to ~75% and YELLOW BOX DISAPPEARS
- Support is now STRONG
- → BULLISH pressure confirmed
- Market may break resistance and reverse from EOR+1
- (WTB disappearing = bullish pressure on resistance)

---

### WTB at RESISTANCE (Example: Resistance=24500, WTB at 24400 = 83.29%)

SCENARIO 1: WTB % INCREASING (83.29% → 90% → 99%)
- Resistance AT 24500 will shift DOWN to 24400
- Market will reverse from 24400 (not 24500 anymore)
- → BEARISH (resistance moving down = closer to current price)
- After shift: Yellow box at 24500 = NATURAL WEAKNESS → IGNORE IT

SCENARIO 2: WTB % DECREASING (83.29% → 80% → 78%)
- Resistance at 24500 becoming STRONGER
- Market reverses from 24500 (WTB at 24400 losing prominence)
- → BULLISH (resistance confirming hold at 24500)

SCENARIO 3: WTB % drops to ~75% and YELLOW BOX DISAPPEARS
- Resistance is now STRONG → BULLISH pressure
- Probability of resistance breakout INCREASES
- Market expected to reverse from EOR+1

## GAME OF PERCENTAGE — WTT (Weak Towards Top)

### WTT at SUPPORT (Example: Support=24300, WTT at 24400 = 93.65%)

SCENARIO 1: WTT % INCREASING (93.65% → 95% → 99%)
- Support at 24300 will shift UP to 24400
- Market will reverse from 24400 (not 24300) — doesn't fall as far
- → BULLISH pressure increasing
- After shift: Yellow box at 24300 = NATURAL WEAKNESS → IGNORE IT

SCENARIO 2: WTT % DECREASING (93.65% → 90% → 84%)
- Support at 24300 becoming STRONGER
- Market expected to fall to 24300 (not reverse at 24400)
- → BEARISH (bullish attempt at support is weakening)

SCENARIO 3: WTT % drops to ~75% and YELLOW BOX DISAPPEARS
- Support is now STRONG → BEARISH pressure
- Probability of support breakdown INCREASES
- Market expected to reverse from EOS-1

---

### WTT at RESISTANCE (Example: Resistance=24100, WTT at 24200 = 87.33%)

SCENARIO 1: WTT % INCREASING (87.33% → 90% → 99%)
- Resistance at 24100 will shift UP to 24200
- Market will reverse from 24200 (not 24100) — breakout threshold higher
- → BULLISH (resistance moving up = harder to reach)
- After shift: Yellow box at 24100 = NATURAL WEAKNESS → IGNORE IT

SCENARIO 2: WTT % DECREASING (87.33% → 80% → 77%)
- Resistance at 24100 becoming STRONGER
- Market reverses from 24100 (will not reach 24200)
- → BEARISH (resistance strengthening at lower level)

SCENARIO 3: WTT % drops to ~75% and YELLOW BOX DISAPPEARS
- Resistance is now STRONG → BEARISH pressure
- Market expected to reverse from EOS-1 (support breakdown risk increases)

## NATURAL WEAKNESS RULE (ALWAYS IGNORE):
After any shift occurs, the OLD level shows a yellow box = NATURAL WEAKNESS.
This is NOT a new WTB/WTT signal. IGNORE IT.
Only treat it as real if the yellow box first disappears AND then reappears at that same level.
