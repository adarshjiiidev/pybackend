# Game of Percentage & State of Confusion

## What is Game of Percentage?

**Definition:** Tracking the **CHANGE** in percentage of the Second Highest Value (WTB/WTT) over time to predict pressure.

**Why it matters:** 
Instead of just saying "It is WTB", we ask "Is WTB getting STRONGER or WEAKER?"

## The Percentage Rules

### 1. WTT Percentage Increasing = BULLISH
- **Scenario:** Weakness towards TOP is growing (e.g., 75% → 80% → 90%).
- **Meaning:** Crowd is moving UP.
- **Pressure:** BULLISH.

### 2. WTT Percentage Decreasing = BEARISH
- **Scenario:** Weakness towards TOP is shrinking (e.g., 90% → 80% → 75%).
- **Meaning:** Crowd failed to move UP. Resistance becoming Strong.
- **Pressure:** BEARISH.

### 3. WTB Percentage Increasing = BEARISH
- **Scenario:** Weakness towards BOTTOM is growing (e.g., 75% → 80% → 90%).
- **Meaning:** Crowd is moving DOWN.
- **Pressure:** BEARISH.

### 4. WTB Percentage Decreasing = BULLISH
- **Scenario:** Weakness towards BOTTOM is shrinking (e.g., 90% → 80% → 75%).
- **Meaning:** Crowd failed to move DOWN. Support becoming Strong.
- **Pressure:** BULLISH.

### 5. Stable Percentage = Range Bound
- **Scenario:** Percentage stays same (e.g., 82% → 82% → 81% → 82%).
- **Meaning:** No clear winner.
- **Pressure:** Market stuck in range.



## Advanced Scenarios (Pressure Combinations)

### Double Bullish (Bull Run)
1. **Top Side:** WTT Increasing (Bullish) OR Top Shift
2. **Bottom Side:** WTB Decreasing (Bullish) OR Top Shift
- **Result:** Strong UP move.

### Double Bearish (Blood Bath)
1. **Top Side:** WTT Decreasing (Bearish) OR Bottom Shift
2. **Bottom Side:** WTB Increasing (Bearish) OR Bottom Shift
- **Result:** Strong DOWN move.

### Conflicting Pressures (9th Scenario / 8th Scenario)
- **Support:** Bullish Pressure (e.g., Strong)
- **Resistance:** Bearish Pressure (e.g., WTT Decreasing)
- **Result:** Market caught in between (Scenario 8 or 9).
