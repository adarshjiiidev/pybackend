# State of Confusion (SOC) on LTP Calculator

## Introduction
State of Confusion is a concept covered in the LTP Calculator free course. It describes the specific market scenarios where the market moves AGAINST the Chart of Accuracy 1.0. Understanding SOC helps traders know when the usual rules of strong support/resistance will be overridden.

SOC is of three kinds — one hour SOC, two hour SOC, and three hour SOC. In SOC, the market moves against the Chart of Accuracy 1.0.

The game of percentage and the TIME PASSED from when the support or resistance was identified as WTT or WTB play a KEY ROLE in the state of confusion.

## Definition
State of Confusion (SOC) occurs when WTT or WTB condition persists for a certain time with specific conditions.
SOC causes the market to move AGAINST the Chart of Accuracy 1.0.
SOC can NEVER occur in Strong support or resistance — only in WTT or WTB conditions.

---

## State of Confusion in SUPPORT (makes market BEARISH)

SOC in support always makes the market BEARISH — market falls from EOR to below.

### Condition 1 — ONE HOUR SOC:
- Support is WTT or WTB
- Its percentage remains STABLE (fluctuating between 2% to 5%) for MORE THAN ONE HOUR
- Resistance must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in support → market turns BEARISH

### Condition 2 — TWO HOUR SOC:
- Support is WTT or WTB
- Its percentage keeps fluctuating between 10% and 15% (but support does NOT shift) for TWO HOURS
- Resistance must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in support → market turns BEARISH

### Condition 3 — THREE HOUR SOC:
- Support has shifted (from top to bottom or bottom to top)
- In that continuation, new support appears as WTT or WTB
- Percentage of this new WTT/WTB becomes stable OR keeps fluctuating (not becoming strong) for MORE THAN THREE HOURS
- Resistance must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in support → market turns BEARISH

### How far does market fall in SOC (Support)?
- Market FALLS FROM: Extension of Resistance (EOR)
- Market BOTTOM IS: Diversion of the SMALLEST strike price in the confusion range
- Example: If support confused between 23800 and 23900 → bottom = diversion of 23800 (smallest)
- Conclusion: Top = EOR | Bottom = diversion of smallest strike in SOC range

### IMPORTANT NOTES for Support SOC:
- Time counting starts from when support FIRST became WTT or WTB (shifting process begins)
- If resistance is also WTT or WTB (not strong), market will NOT fall → no SOC effect

---

## State of Confusion in RESISTANCE (makes market BULLISH)

SOC in resistance always makes the market BULLISH — market rises from EOS to above.

### Condition 1 — ONE HOUR SOC:
- Resistance is WTT or WTB
- Its percentage remains STABLE (fluctuating between 2% to 5%) for MORE THAN ONE HOUR
- Support must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in resistance → market turns BULLISH

### Condition 2 — TWO HOUR SOC:
- Resistance is WTT or WTB
- Its percentage keeps fluctuating between 10% and 15% (but resistance does NOT shift) for TWO HOURS
- Support must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in resistance → market turns BULLISH

### Condition 3 — THREE HOUR SOC:
- Resistance has shifted (from top to bottom or bottom to top)
- In that continuation, new resistance appears as WTT or WTB
- Percentage of this new WTT/WTB becomes stable OR keeps fluctuating (not becoming strong) for MORE THAN THREE HOURS
- Support must be STRONG
→ Result: SOC in resistance → market turns BULLISH

### How far does market rise in SOC (Resistance)?
- Market RISES FROM: Extension of Support (EOS)
- Market TOP IS: Diversion of the LARGEST strike price in the confusion range
- Example: If resistance confused between 23900 and 24000 → top = diversion of 24000 (largest)
- Conclusion: Bottom = EOS | Top = diversion of largest strike in SOC range

### IMPORTANT NOTES for Resistance SOC:
- Time counting starts from when resistance FIRST became WTT or WTB
- If support is also WTT or WTB (not strong), market will NOT fly → no SOC effect

## SOC Summary Table

| SOC In     | Market Direction | Starting Point | Target              | Requirement        |
|------------|-----------------|----------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Support    | BEARISH (falls) | EOR            | Diversion of smallest SOC strike | Resistance must be STRONG |
| Resistance | BULLISH (rises) | EOS            | Diversion of largest SOC strike  | Support must be STRONG    |

## Time Conditions:
| Condition | % Stability    | Duration  |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| 1-Hour    | 2% to 5%       | >1 hour   |
| 2-Hour    | 10% to 15%     | ~2 hours  |
| 3-Hour    | Stable/varying | >3 hours  |

Note: Time counted from beginning of shifting (when WTT/WTB first appeared).
