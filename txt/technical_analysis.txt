# Technical Analysis Concepts

## Moving Averages

### Simple Moving Average (SMA)
- Average price over N periods
- Equal weight to all periods
- Common periods: 20, 50, 100, 200

### Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
- More weight to recent prices
- Responds faster to price changes
- Popular in day trading

### Key Signals
- **Golden Cross**: 50 SMA crosses above 200 SMA (Bullish)
- **Death Cross**: 50 SMA crosses below 200 SMA (Bearish)
- Price above MA = Bullish
- Price below MA = Bearish

## Momentum Indicators

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- Measures overbought/oversold conditions
- Range: 0-100
- **> 70**: Overbought (potential reversal down)
- **< 30**: Oversold (potential reversal up)
- Best with divergence confirmation

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- MACD line = 12 EMA - 26 EMA
- Signal line = 9 EMA of MACD
- Histogram = MACD - Signal
- **Bullish**: MACD crosses above signal
- **Bearish**: MACD crosses below signal

### Stochastic Oscillator
- Compares closing price to price range
- Range: 0-100
- **> 80**: Overbought
- **< 20**: Oversold
- Use %K and %D crossovers

## Volume Indicators

### Volume Profile
- Shows volume traded at each price level
- High volume areas = Support/Resistance
- Point of Control (POC) = Highest volume level

### OBV (On-Balance Volume)
- Running total of volume
- Rising OBV = Buying pressure
- Falling OBV = Selling pressure
- Divergence signals trend change

### VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
- Average price weighted by volume
- Used for intraday trading
- Price above VWAP = Bullish
- Price below VWAP = Bearish

## Chart Patterns

### Reversal Patterns

**Head and Shoulders**
- Three peaks, middle highest
- Bearish reversal pattern
- Neckline break confirms pattern

**Inverse Head and Shoulders**
- Three troughs, middle lowest
- Bullish reversal pattern

**Double Top/Bottom**
- Two similar highs/lows
- Reversal patterns
- Break of support/resistance confirms

### Continuation Patterns

**Flags and Pennants**
- Brief consolidation in trend
- Breakout continues original trend

**Triangles** (Ascending, Descending, Symmetrical)
- Converging trendlines
- Breakout direction varies

**Rectangles**
- Horizontal support and resistance
- Consolidation before continuation

## Support and Resistance

### Identification
- Previous highs/lows
- Psychological levels (round numbers)
- Fibonacci retracement levels
- Pivot points

### Characteristics
- **Multiple touches** = Stronger level
- **Volume** at level confirms strength
- **Broken support becomes resistance** (vice versa)

### Fibonacci Retracement
Key levels: 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%
- Used to find support in uptrend
- Used to find resistance in downtrend

## Candlestick Patterns

### Bullish Patterns
- **Hammer**: Long lower wick, small body
- **Bullish Engulfing**: Large green candle engulfs previous red
- **Morning Star**: 3-candle reversal pattern
- **Piercing Pattern**: Green candle closes above midpoint of previous red

### Bearish Patterns
- **Shooting Star**: Long upper wick, small body
- **Bearish Engulfing**: Large red candle engulfs previous green
- **Evening Star**: 3-candle reversal pattern
- **Dark Cloud Cover**: Red candle closes below midpoint of previous green

### Indecision Patterns
- **Doji**: Open = Close (or very close)
- **Spinning Top**: Small body, long wicks both sides

## Trend Analysis

### Trend Identification
- **Uptrend**: Higher highs + Higher lows
- **Downtrend**: Lower highs + Lower lows
- **Sideways**: Horizontal movement

### Trendlines
- Connect swing lows in uptrend
- Connect swing highs in downtrend
- Minimum 2 touches to draw
- 3rd touch validates trendline

### Channels
- Parallel lines containing price action
- Trade within channel boundaries
- Breakout signals trend change

## Volatility Indicators

### Bollinger Bands
- Middle: 20 SMA
- Upper/Lower: ±2 standard deviations
- **Squeeze**: Low volatility, breakout imminent
- **Expansion**: High volatility
- Price touching bands = Potential reversal

### ATR (Average True Range)
- Measures volatility
- Higher ATR = Higher volatility
- Used for stop loss placement
- Not directional

## Best Practices

1. **Confluence**: Use multiple indicators
2. **Time Frames**: Check higher time frame for trend
3. **Risk Management**: Always use stop losses
4. **Backtesting**: Test strategies before live trading
5. **Keep Learning**: Markets evolve, strategies must adapt
6. **Psychology**: Discipline > Indicators
