# Shifting Pressure & Rules

## What is Shifting?

**Definition:** When the **Second Highest** value (OI or Volume) becomes the **Highest** value at a new strike price.

**Simple:** The "Boss" (Support/Resistance) moves to a new house.

**Sheesh Aasan Analogy:**
- The biggest group of people PUSHING moved to a new spot.

## The 4 Golden Rules of Shifting

### 1. Shift to TOP Side = BULLISH (Bull Control)

**Action:** Support or Resistance moves from a smaller number to a larger number.
**Analogy:** Pushing group moved UP (towards Head). Market follows them.

**Example:**
- Resistance shifts from 17500 → 17550
- Support shifts from 17400 → 17450

**Result:** Market goes UP.

### 2. Shift to BOTTOM Side = BEARISH (Bear Control)

**Action:** Support or Resistance moves from a larger number to a smaller number.
**Analogy:** Pushing group moved DOWN (towards Legs). Market follows them.

**Example:**
- Resistance shifts from 17600 → 17550
- Support shifts from 17500 → 17450

**Result:** Market goes DOWN.

### 3. Shift & Strong = CONFIRMED Trend

**Scenario:** Shifting happens AND the new level becomes **Strong**.
**Result:** Very clear trend signal.

- **Bottom Shift + Strong = Clear BEARISH** (Market will fall)
- **Top Shift + Strong = Clear BULLISH** (Market will rise)

### 4. Shift & Reverse (Wapas Shift) = Pressure REVERSES

**Scenario:** It shifted, but then shifted back to the original place.
**Result:** The pressure FLIPS immediately.

- **Bearish to Bullish Example:**
  1. Shift Bottom (17500 → 17450) [Bearish]
  2. Shift BACK (17450 → 17500) [Now Bullish!]

## Shifting Combination Scenarios

### 1. Bull Run Scenario
**Conditions:** 
1. Resistance shifts TOP (or is WTT)
2. Support shifts TOP (or is WTT)
3. OR Shifted Top and shows WTT *again*

**Result:** Market keeps breaking resistance levels.

### 2. Blood Bath Scenario
**Conditions:**
1. Resistance shifts BOTTOM (or is WTB)
2. Support shifts BOTTOM (or is WTB)
3. OR Shifted Bottom and shows WTB *again*

**Result:** Market keeps breaking support levels.

## Natural Weakness Exception (The "Ghost" Rule)

**Concept:** When the "Boss" moves house, some luggage is left behind at the old house.

**Rule:** 
If shifting moves FROM 17500 TO 17550:
- The leftover OI/Volume at 17500 might look like WTB/WTT.
- This is **NATURAL WEAKNESS**.
- **IGNORE IT.** Do not treat it as a new trade signal.

**Example:**
1. Resistance at 17500.
2. Shifts to 17550.
3. Now 17500 shows "WTB" (Weakness towards bottom).
4. **Conclusion:** Ignore. It's just the leftover crowd leaving.
