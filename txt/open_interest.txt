# Open Interest on AI LTP Calculator

## What is Open Interest?
Open Interest (OI) = Total number of OUTSTANDING contracts (long or short) that have NOT been closed or settled.
- OI increases when new contracts are created
- OI decreases when positions are closed/squared off
- Open positions of WRITERS create Open Interest

## Who is captured by OI on each side?
- CALL SIDE OI = CALL WRITERS = BEARISH players (they profit when market goes DOWN)
- PUT SIDE OI = PUT WRITERS = BULLISH players (they profit when market goes UP)

Logic:
- If you BUY a call → you are BULLISH. The person who SOLD it to you is BEARISH.
- Hence Call writers (sellers) = Bears. Call OI = Bearish positions.
- If you BUY a put → you are BEARISH. The person who SOLD it to you is BULLISH.
- Hence Put writers (sellers) = Bulls. Put OI = Bullish positions.

## Key Properties of Open Interest:
- OI is POSITIONAL data (does not reset daily)
- Starts from where it was at previous trading session's close
- Can be minimum ZERO, never negative
- Increases: new contracts created | Decreases: positions closed by writers
- OI Change is more useful than raw OI for intraday direction

---

## What is Open Interest Change?
OI Change = Current OI − Last trading day's closing OI
- Intraday data — resets to 0 at market open (9:15 AM) every day
- Can be positive, negative, or zero

### OI Change Text Color on LTP Calculator:
- GREEN text = OI Change positive = Current OI > last close OI = NEW POSITIONS CREATED
- RED text = OI Change negative = Current OI < last close OI = WRITERS SQUARING OFF (closing positions)

## How Volume and OI Form on Option Chain (step by step)

| Time  | Action                        | OI Change | Volume | OI     |
|-------|-------------------------------|-----------|--------|--------|
| 3:30 PM (prev day close) | —             | —         | —      | 83,917 |
| 9:15 AM (market open)   | No trades yet  | 0         | 0      | 83,917 |
| 9:30 AM | 83 lots: writers sold, buyers bought | +83   | 83     | 84,000 |
| 9:45 AM | Writers square off 4,965 lots; buyers trade among themselves | -4,882 | 9,048  | 79,118 |
| 9:55 AM | More writers square off; no buyer-to-buyer trade | -8,913 | 13,079 | 75,004 |
| 10:05 AM | Writers create 11,507 new positions | +2,594 | 24,586 | 77,598 |

Volume = Total contracts traded by ANYONE (buyer or writer)
OI Change = Only contracts created/closed by WRITERS

Insight: When Volume > OI change, buyers are trading among themselves (no new writer positions).
When Volume = OI change, only writers are active.

---

## PCR (Put-Call Ratio)
PCR = Total Put OI / Total Call OI
- PCR > 1: More bullish positions (more put writers = more bulls) → BULLISH bias
- PCR < 1: More bearish positions (more call writers = more bears) → BEARISH bias
- PCR = 1: Balanced market

## Open Interest vs Volume (key differences)
| Feature        | Open Interest              | Volume                      |
|----------------|---------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Resets daily?  | NO (positional data)      | YES (intraday data)         |
| Who creates it?| Writers only              | Anyone (buyers + writers)   |
| Can be negative?| No (min = 0)             | No (min = 0)                |
| Used in LTP?   | Yes (with Volume for S/R) | Yes (with OI for S/R)       |
