# General Finance and Trading Terms

## Basic Market Terms

### Stock/Share
Represents ownership in a company. When you buy a stock, you become a partial owner.

### Index
A collection of stocks representing a market segment
- **Nifty 50**: Top 50 companies on NSE
- **Bank Nifty**: Banking sector index
- **Sensex**: Top 30 companies on BSE

### Market Capitalization
Total value of company's shares = Share Price × Total Shares
- **Large Cap**: > ₹20,000 crores
- **Mid Cap**: ₹5,000 - ₹20,000 crores
- **Small Cap**: < ₹5,000 crores

### Volume
Number of shares traded in a period
- High volume = High liquidity
- Volume confirms price movements

### Volatility
Measure of price fluctuations
- High volatility = Higher risk & potential reward
- Low volatility = Stable prices

## Fundamental Analysis Terms

### P/E Ratio (Price to Earnings)
Market Price / Earnings Per Share
- **High P/E**: Overvalued or high growth expected
- **Low P/E**: Undervalued or poor growth prospects
- Compare within same sector

### EPS (Earnings Per Share)
Net Profit / Total Shares
- Higher EPS = Better profitability

### ROE (Return on Equity)
Net Income / Shareholder's Equity × 100
- Measures company's profitability
- **> 15%**: Generally good

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio
Total Debt / Total Equity
- **< 1**: Healthy balance
- **> 2**: High leverage, risky

### Book Value
Company's total assets minus liabilities
- Market Price < Book Value = Potentially undervalued

## Trading Terms

### Bid and Ask
- **Bid**: Highest price buyers willing to pay
- **Ask**: Lowest price sellers willing to accept
- **Spread**: Difference between bid and ask

### Market Order
Buy/sell immediately at current market price
- Executes fast
- Price not guaranteed

### Limit Order
Buy/sell only at specified price or better
- Price control
- May not execute

### Stop Loss
Automatic sell order when price falls to specified level
- Risk management tool
- Protects capital

### Take Profit
Automatic sell order when price reaches target
- Lock in profits
- Disciplined exit

### Intraday Trading
Buy and sell within same trading day
- No overnight risk
- Requires quick decisions
- Higher brokerage impact

### Swing Trading
Hold positions for days to weeks
- Capture medium-term moves
- Less screen time than intraday

### Position Trading
Long-term holding (months to years)
- Based on fundamentals
- Ride major trends

## Market Timings (India)

### NSE/BSE Regular Session
- **Pre-open**: 9:00 AM - 9:15 AM
- **Trading**: 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM
- **Closing**: 3:30 PM - 4:00 PM (for order matching)

### After Market Orders (AMO)
Place orders after market hours for next day execution

## Order Types

### CNC (Cash and Carry)
Delivery trading - hold stocks in demat account

### MIS (Margin Intraday Square-off)
Intraday with margin
- Auto square-off by broker if not closed

### BO (Bracket Order)
Advanced intraday order with auto stop-loss and target

### CO (Cover Order)
Intraday order with mandatory stop-loss

## Dividend and Corporate Actions

### Dividend
Company's profit distributed to shareholders
- **Ex-dividend Date**: After this, no dividend for new buyers

### Bonus Shares
Free additional shares to existing shareholders
- Example: 1:1 bonus = 1 free share for each held

### Stock Split
Divide share into smaller denominations
- Example: 1:2 split = 1 share becomes 2, price halves
- Increases liquidity

### Rights Issue
Offer to buy additional shares at discounted price
- Existing shareholders get preference

## Risk Management

### Position Sizing
Amount of capital allocated per trade
- Never risk > 2% per trade

### Risk-Reward Ratio
Potential Profit / Potential Loss
- Minimum 1:2 recommended (risk ₹100 to make ₹200)

### Diversification
Don't put all eggs in one basket
- Spread across sectors
- Mix of large/mid/small caps

### Hedging
Use derivatives to reduce risk
- Buy put options to protect long positions
- Create collar strategies

## Market Indicators

### FII/DII Activity
- **FII**: Foreign Institutional Investors
- **DII**: Domestic Institutional Investors
- Net buying = Bullish, Net selling = Bearish

### VIX (Volatility Index)
Measures market fear/uncertainty
- **Low VIX < 15**: Calm market
- **High VIX > 25**: Fearful market

### Advance-Decline Ratio
Number of advancing stocks / Declining stocks
- **> 1**: Bullish market breadth
- **< 1**: Bearish market breadth

## Account Types

### Demat Account
Holds shares in electronic form
- Mandatory for trading

### Trading Account  
Used to place buy/sell orders
- Linked to demat account

### Savings Account
Bank account linked for fund transfers

## Taxation (India - Basic Overview)

### Short-term Capital Gains (STCG)
Holding < 1 year
- **15%** on equity delivery

### Long-term Capital Gains (LTCG)
Holding > 1 year
- **10%** on gains > ₹1 lakh (equity)

### Intraday Trading
Treated as business income, taxed per slab

**Note**: Tax laws change, consult CA for current rates
