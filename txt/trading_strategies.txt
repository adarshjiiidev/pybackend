# Trading Strategies & 9:20 AM Rule

## The 9:20 AM Strategy

**Best For:** Beginners.
**Time:** Starts at 9:20 AM (Market settles initial volatility).

### How it works:
1. Open LTP Calculator Chart at 9:20 AM.
2. The AI plots **4 Magical Lines**:
   - **EOR+1** (Top most)
   - **EOR** (Resistance)
   - **EOS** (Support)
   - **EOS-1** (Bottom most)

### Trading Rules:
- **No Brainer:** Do not analyze deeply. Just follow the lines.
- **If Market hits EOR or EOR+1:**
  - Trade: **BUY PUT** (Bearish trade)
  - Assumption: Market will reverse down.
- **If Market hits EOS or EOS-1:**
  - Trade: **BUY CALL** (Bullish trade)
  - Assumption: Market will reverse up.

### Risk Management (Stoploss & Targets):
- **Trade from EOR/EOS:**
  - Stoploss: ~110 points (Option chart or Spot? Usually Spot deviation).
  - Target: 50 points.
- **Trade from EOR+1/EOS-1 (Safe):**
  - Stoploss: ~60 points.
  - Target: 100 points.
- **Averaging:**
  - If took trade at **EOR** -> Market goes up -> Average at **EOR+1** (Double quantity).
  - If took trade at **EOS** -> Market goes down -> Average at **EOS-1**.

## Reversal Trading (EOR/EOS)

**Concept:** LTP Calculator specializes in "Reversal Trading" (Catching top/bottom) rather than breakout trading.

### 6 Kinds of Reversals:
1. **EOR** (Extension of Resistance)
2. **EOS** (Extension of Support)
3. **EOR+1** (Divergence above Resistance)
4. **EOS-1** (Divergence below Support)
5. **Diversion** (Mid-points based on strike gaps)
6. **End of Diversion** (Last possible reversal point)

### Calculation:
Reversal points are calculated based on Option Chain Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega) to find the precise turning point.

**Example:**
- Strike: 17500
- LTP Calculator shows EOR: 17523.
- Market often touches 17523 and reverses precisely.

## Diversion Trading logic

- **Call Side Diversion:** Selling opportunity (Bearish).
- **Put Side Diversion:** Buying opportunity (Bullish).
- Always check **COA 2.0** (OI Change) at diversion points to confirm strength.
