# Weekly Range on LTP Calculator

## What is Weekly Range?
Weekly Range = The range within which the market (index) is EXPECTED TO CLOSE on weekly expiry day.
- Applies ONLY to indices with weekly expiry (e.g., NIFTY 50, BANK NIFTY).
- Three levels: L1 (risky), L2 (moderate), L3 (safe)
- Most useful for OPTION WRITERS.

---

## How to Calculate Weekly Range

### STEP 1: When to capture data
- On the FIRST trading day of the weekly expiry week
- At exactly 9:15 AM (market open)
- Open the option chain for the CURRENT weekly expiry

### STEP 2: Note these three values
1. CMP = Current Market Price (imaginary line)
2. First OTM LTP on CALL side (first Out-of-The-Money call LTP)
3. First OTM LTP on PUT side (first Out-of-The-Money put LTP)

Example:
- CMP = 23432.20
- First OTM Call LTP = 163.10
- First OTM Put LTP = 121.05

### STEP 3: Calculate the SUM of OTM LTPs
SUM = OTM Call LTP + OTM Put LTP = 163.10 + 121.05 = 284.15
(This SUM value is used for all three range calculations)

---

## L1 Weekly Range (RISKY — market CAN move beyond this)
FORMULA:
- L1 Support = CMP − SUM = 23432.20 − 284.15 = 23148.05
- L1 Resistance = CMP + SUM = 23432.20 + 284.15 = 23716.35
- Range: ~23148 to ~23716

Meaning: On expiry day, NIFTY may close somewhere between L1 support and L1 resistance.
Risk: Market CAN move outside L1 range.

## L2 Weekly Range (MODERATE — market generally stays within)
FORMULA (built from L1):
- L2 Support = L1 Support − SUM = 23148.05 − 284.15 = 21863.90 (in example uses 22148.05)
- L2 Resistance = L1 Resistance + SUM = 23716.35 + 284.15 = 24000.50
- Range: ~21864 to ~24001

Meaning: Market GENERALLY does not move beyond L2. More reliable than L1.

## L3 Weekly Range (SAFE — very low probability of breach)
FORMULA (built from L2):
- L3 Support = L2 Support − SUM
- L3 Resistance = L2 Resistance + SUM
- Very wide range — highly unlikely to be breached

---

## Option Writing Strategy with Weekly Range

Goal: Sell strike prices OUTSIDE the weekly range for lower-risk premium collection.

### Basic Strategy:
- Above L1 Resistance → Sell CALL options (those calls will expire worthless)
- Below L1 Support → Sell PUT options (those puts will expire worthless)
- Always write in HEDGED positions (sell both sides simultaneously)

### Implementation:
1. Number strike prices outside the weekly range on each side (1st, 2nd, 3rd...)
2. Start with 1 lot for 1st strike outside range on each side (hedged pair)
3. Increase quantity as you go further OTM (based on risk capacity)
4. Stop-loss for each pair = SUM of Call LTP + Put LTP for that pair

### Level-by-Level:
- 1st level outside range (e.g., 17650 CE + 17200 PE): 1 lot each, SL = sum of their LTPs
- 2nd level (17700 CE + 17150 PE): 1 lot each, SL = sum of their LTPs
- 3rd level (17750 CE + 17100 PE): 1 lot each, SL = sum of their LTPs
- Strategy: When stop-loss hit on one side → book that side, keep other side (it expires at zero)
- It is very rare for BOTH sides to hit SL simultaneously

### Risk Levels:
| Range | Risk Level | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| L1    | RISKY     | Market can breach — for aggressive writers |
| L2    | MODERATE  | Market generally stays within — safer |
| L3    | SAFE      | Very low breach probability — most conservative |
