# Weekly Range (LTP Calculator Feature)

## Definition
The **Weekly Range** defines the probable boundaries for the market based on option premiums. It helps traders know where the market is likely to stop or reverse.

## Key Components
Three levels of Support and Resistance:
1. **L1 (Level 1):** 33% Probability of market stopping here.
2. **L2 (Level 2):** 66% Probability of market stopping here.
3. **L3 (Level 3):** 99% Probability of market stopping here. (Maximum Range).

## Calculation Method
The range is calculated using the **Sum of First ITM (In-The-Money)** premiums from both Call and Put sides.

**Reference Time:** 
- Taken from the **Last Expiry** (usually Tuesday closing).
- The First ITM Call + First ITM Put sum is recorded.

**Formula:**
`X = Sum of First ITM Call + First ITM Put (from last expiry)`

**Next Day Projection (Where Market Opens):**
If market opens at Price `P`:
- **Resistance L1:** P + (1 * X)
- **Resistance L2:** P + (2 * X)
- **Resistance L3:** P + (3 * X)

- **Support L1:** P - (1 * X)
- **Support L2:** P - (2 * X)
- **Support L3:** P - (3 * X)

## Trading Usage
- **Buying:** Safe to buy at Support L2 or L3.
- **Selling:** Safe to sell at Resistance L2 or L3.
- **L1:** Risky / Minor hurdle.
- **L3 (99%):** The "Iron Wall". Market rarely crosses this in a normal week.
