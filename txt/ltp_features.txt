## Greeks on AI LTP Calculator

Available on the option chain:
- **Delta (Δ)** — Rate of change of option price with respect to underlying price
- **Gamma (Γ)** — Rate of change of Delta
- **Theta (Θ)** — Time decay — how much option value decreases each day
- **Vega (ν)** — Sensitivity to volatility (IV)
- **IV (Implied Volatility)** — Market's expectation of future volatility
