# Support and Resistance on LTP Calculator

## Core Definition (Dr. Vinay Prakash Tiwari's theory)

"Starting from the PAIR OF IMAGINARY LINE STRIKE PRICES and moving towards the OTM strike prices,
the HIGHEST VOLUME or HIGHEST OPEN INTEREST, whichever is CLOSEST TO THE IMAGINARY LINE,
is called the SUPPORT on the PUT side and RESISTANCE on the CALL side."

Key points:
- RESISTANCE = Call side (always)
- SUPPORT = Put side (always)
- Both VOLUME and OPEN INTEREST matter (unlike most traders who use only OI)
- The factor closest to the imaginary line wins

---

## How to Find Support

Step 1: Identify the pair of imaginary line strike prices (e.g., 23050 and 23100 if CMP is ~23075)
Step 2: Start from the ATM pair and move TOWARDS OTM on the PUT side (23050 → 23000 → 22950...)
Step 3: Find the HIGHEST VOLUME (use BLUE color) and HIGHEST OI (use GREEN color on put side)
Step 4: Whichever is CLOSEST to the imaginary line = SUPPORT
- If both highest Vol and highest OI are at the same strike → support is of BOTH Volume and OI
- If highest Vol is closer → support is Volume-based
- If highest OI is closer → support is OI-based

## How to Find Resistance

Step 1: Identify the pair of imaginary line strike prices
Step 2: Start from the ATM pair and move TOWARDS OTM on the CALL side (23100 → 23150 → 23200...)
Step 3: Find the HIGHEST VOLUME (use BLUE color) and HIGHEST OI (use PINK color on call side)
Step 4: Whichever is CLOSEST to the imaginary line = RESISTANCE
- If both in same strike → resistance is of BOTH Volume and OI

---

## Color Quick Reference for Finding S/R:

CALL SIDE (Resistance):
- PINK = Highest OI (primary resistance OI indicator)
- BLUE = Highest Volume (primary resistance Volume indicator)

PUT SIDE (Support):
- GREEN = Highest OI (primary support OI indicator)
- BLUE = Highest Volume (primary support Volume indicator)

## About Support and Resistance on Traditional vs LTP Calculator:

Traditional method: Based on historical price action (trendlines, previous highs/lows)
LTP Calculator method: Based on LIVE option chain data (Volume + Open Interest)
- LTP Calculator is more dynamic — updates in real time
- Based on actual smart money positions (writers = institutional players)
- More accurate for intraday Indian markets

---

## Three Conditions of Support/Resistance
1. STRONG — Second highest value is below 75% of highest. No yellow box. Stable.
2. WTT (Weak Towards Top) — Yellow box at HIGHER strike. Trying to shift up.
3. WTB (Weak Towards Bottom) — Yellow box at LOWER strike. Trying to shift down.

---

## Open Interest Interpretation:
- CALL SIDE OI = BEARISH positions (call writers are bearish)
- PUT SIDE OI = BULLISH positions (put writers are bullish)
- Increasing OI (green text) = new positions being created
- Decreasing OI (red text) = writers squaring off (unwinding)
