# Support and Resistance - The Sheesh Aasan Concept

## Understanding The LTP Calculator Layout

### The Sheesh Aasan (Headstand) Analogy

**Imagine the market is doing Sheesh Aasan (headstand):**

```
HEAD (Top) ← Larger Strike Prices (17700, 17750, 17800...)
  ↑
BODY (Middle) ← Current Market Price (Imaginary Line)
  ↓
LEGS (Bottom) ← Smaller Strike Prices (17300, 17250, 17200...)
```

**Why this matters:**
- In LTP Calculator, counting goes 1, 2, 3, 4... (straight counting)
- Smaller strike prices (17200, 17250) are at BOTTOM/LEGS
- Larger strike prices (17700, 17750) are at TOP/HEAD
- Market stands upside down in option chain!

### The People Pushing Analogy

**Imagine two groups of people pushing:**

**RESISTANCE (Call Side - Top/Head):**
- People pushing market DOWN from head side
- They want market to go lower
- More people here = more downward pressure

**SUPPORT (Put Side - Bottom/Legs):**
- People pushing market UP from leg side  
- They want market to go higher
- More people here = more upward pressure

## How It Works

### Scenario 1: Support is Stronger
```
Resistance (Head): 50 people pushing DOWN
        ↓↓↓
    Market @ 17500
        ↑↑↑↑↑
Support (Legs): 100 people pushing UP

Result: Market goes UP! (More people pushing from bottom)
```

**Example:**
- Support at 17450 has 10 lakh OI
- Resistance at 17550 has 5 lakh OI
- Support has MORE people → Market will move TOWARDS RESISTANCE (upward)

### Scenario 2: Resistance is Stronger
```
Resistance (Head): 100 people pushing DOWN
        ↓↓↓↓↓
    Market @ 17500
        ↑↑↑
Support (Legs): 50 people pushing UP

Result: Market goes DOWN! (More people pushing from top)
```

**Example:**
- Resistance at 17550 has 12 lakh OI
- Support at 17450 has 6 lakh OI
- Resistance has MORE people → Market will move TOWARDS SUPPORT (downward)

## Support and Resistance - Technical Definition

### Support (Put Side - Bottom/Legs)

**Location:** Below current market price (smaller strike prices)  
**Direction:** Calculated from LARGE strike to SMALL strike moving toward imaginary line  
**Formula:** Highest OI/Volume nearest to imaginary line on PUT side

**Example:**
```
Imaginary Line: 17500
Put Strikes: 17400 (8L OI), 17450 (12L OI), 17500 (10L OI)

Moving from large to small toward line:
17500 → 17450 → 17400
         ↑
    12 Lakh is closest and highest
    
Support = 17450 ✓
```

### Resistance (Call Side - Top/Head)

**Location:** Above current market price (larger strike prices)  
**Direction:** Calculated from SMALL strike to LARGE strike moving toward imaginary line  
**Formula:** Highest OI/Volume nearest to imaginary line on CALL side

**Example:**
```
Imaginary Line: 17500
Call Strikes: 17500 (9L OI), 17550 (15L OI), 17600 (7L OI)

Moving from small to large toward line:
17500 → 17550 → 17600
         ↑
    15 Lakh is closest and highest
    
Resistance = 17550 ✓
```

## The Tug of War

**Think of it like this:**

1. **More people pushing from BOTTOM** = Market goes UP (toward head)
2. **More people pushing from TOP** = Market goes DOWN (toward legs)
3. **Equal people both sides** = Market stays in RANGE

**Key Rule:**
- Market moves TOWARD the weaker side
- If support is weaker, market goes down to support
- If resistance is weaker, market goes up to resistance
- If both are strong, market stays between them

## Important Notes

**OI vs Volume:**
- Both OI and Volume create support/resistance
- Always consider whichever is CLOSEST to imaginary line
- If both at same level, they reinforce each other

**Call Side Rules:**
- Smaller strikes are IN THE MONEY (inside/ander)
- Larger strikes are OUT OF THE MONEY (outside/bahar)
- Count from small to large

**Put Side Rules:**
- Larger strikes are IN THE MONEY (inside/ander)
- Smaller strikes are OUT OF THE MONEY (outside/bahar)
- Count from large to small
