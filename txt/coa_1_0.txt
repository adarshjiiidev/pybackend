# Chart of Accuracy 1.0 (COA 1.0)

## Key Terms
- EOR: Extension of Resistance — clicking volume of resistance strike gives exact reversal price
- EOS: Extension of Support — clicking volume of support strike gives exact reversal price
- EOR+1 / EOS+1: ONE STRIKE PRICE ABOVE (towards top, higher strike price)
- EOR-1 / EOS-1: ONE STRIKE PRICE BELOW (towards bottom, lower strike price)
- WTT+1 / WTB+1: One strike above the WTT/WTB strike
- WTT-1 / WTB-1: One strike below the WTT/WTB strike
- NOTE: The option chain is INVERTED — smaller strike prices are at TOP, larger at BOTTOM

---

## The 9 Scenarios (COA 1.1 to COA 1.9)

### COA 1.1 — Scenario 1: Both Strong
- Support: STRONG | Resistance: STRONG
- Market: RANGE BOUND (ideal scenario)
- Top: EOR (Extension of Resistance)
- Bottom: EOS (Extension of Support)
- Trade: Buy Call at EOS, Sell Call/Buy Put at EOR
- Note: First hit to any level is generally safe. Risk increases with repeated hits.

### COA 1.2 — Scenario 2: Support Strong, Resistance WTB
- Support: STRONG | Resistance: WTB (Weak Towards Bottom)
- Market: BEARISH — resistance WTB creates bearish pressure on support, support will break down
- Top: EOR
- Bottom: EOS-1 (one diversion below support — market reverses from diversion next to support)
- Meaning: Resistance shifting down = bearish, market expected to fall below support

### COA 1.3 — Scenario 3: Support Strong, Resistance WTT
- Support: STRONG | Resistance: WTT (Weak Towards Top)
- Market: BULLISH — resistance WTT means resistance will break out upward
- Top: WTT-1 (one strike below the WTT strike — market reverses from diversion prior to WTT)
- Bottom: EOS
- Meaning: Resistance about to shift up = bullish

### COA 1.4 — Scenario 4: Support WTB, Resistance Strong
- Support: WTB | Resistance: STRONG
- Market: BEARISH — support WTB means support will break down
- Top: EOR
- Bottom: WTB+1 (one strike above the WTB strike — market reverses from diversion prior to WTB)
- Meaning: Support shifting down = bearish

### COA 1.5 — Scenario 5: Support WTT, Resistance Strong
- Support: WTT | Resistance: STRONG
- Market: BULLISH — support WTT creates bullish pressure on resistance, resistance will break out
- Top: EOR+1 (one diversion above resistance — market reverses from next diversion to resistance)
- Bottom: EOS
- Meaning: Support shifting up = bullish, resistance about to break

### COA 1.6 — Scenario 6: Both WTB — BLOOD BATH
- Support: WTB | Resistance: WTB
- Market: EXTREME BEARISH — Blood Bath condition
- Top: EOR
- Bottom: N/A (No bottom — free fall)
- NEVER take bullish trades in Blood Bath
- Double bearish pressure = market can fall without bound

### COA 1.7 — Scenario 7: Both WTT — BULL RUN
- Support: WTT | Resistance: WTT
- Market: EXTREME BULLISH — Bull Run condition
- Top: N/A (No top — unlimited upside)
- Bottom: EOS
- NEVER take bearish trades in Bull Run
- Double bullish pressure = market can rise without bound

### COA 1.8 — Scenario 8: Support WTB, Resistance WTT
- Support: WTB | Resistance: WTT
- Market: NOT TRADABLE (conflicting signals)
- Condition: Wait until either support or resistance becomes strong
- This is an expander — both levels pulling in opposite directions

### COA 1.9 — Scenario 9: Support WTT, Resistance WTB
- Support: WTT | Resistance: WTB
- Market: NOT TRADABLE (conflicting signals)
- Condition: Wait until either support or resistance becomes strong
- This is a squeeze — both levels pushing toward center

## DIVERSION RULES (important for finding exact entry levels)
1. Number of diversions = Number of strike prices between support and resistance + 1
2. If no strikes between support and resistance = 1 diversion
3. If support and resistance at same strike price = No diversion (trade extension to extension)
- Bullish motive: Call side = diversions, Put side = End of Diversions
- Bearish motive: Put side = diversions, Call side = End of Diversions
- Click on volume of strike price to get diversion/extension values
