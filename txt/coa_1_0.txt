# COA 1.0 - The 9 Scenarios (Chart of Accuracy)

**COA 1.0** defines market range and direction based on the combination of Support and Resistance states.

## Key Terms
- **EOR:** Extension of Resistance (Sell/Put Level)
- **EOS:** Extension of Support (Buy/Call Level)
- **WTT:** Weak Towards Top
- **WTB:** Weak Towards Bottom

## The 9 Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Both Strong
- **Support:** Strong
- **Resistance:** Strong
- **Trend:** Range Bound
- **Call Entry:** EOS (Buy)
- **Put Entry:** EOR (Sell)
- **Max Range:** EOR to EOS

### Scenario 2: Resistance Strong, Support WTB
- **Support:** Weak Towards Bottom
- **Resistance:** Strong
- **Trend:** Bearish (Pressure Down)
- **Call Entry:** Avoid / risky
- **Put Entry:** EOR (Sell)
- **Target:** EOR to WTB+1 (or broken support)

### Scenario 3: Resistance Strong, Support WTT
- **Support:** Weak Towards Top
- **Resistance:** Strong
- **Trend:** Bullish (Pressure Up)
- **Call Entry:** EOS (Buy)
- **Put Entry:** EOR+1 (Risky) or Avoid
- **Target:** EOS to EOR+1

### Scenario 5: Resistance WTT, Support Strong (Bullish... usually)
- **Standard:** Resistance WTT (Bullish) + Support Strong (Neutral) = **BULLISH**.
- **Nuance (CRITICAL):** If Support became Strong **FROM WTT** (which is Bearish pressure), then:
  - Resistance (Bullish) vs Support (Bearish) = **CONSOLIDATION / SCENARIO 8**.
  - **Rule:** Conflicting pressures ALWAYS equal Consolidation.

### Scenario 8: Bullish Support vs Bearish Resistance (The Expander)
- **Resistance:** WTT (Weak Toward Top) → Bullish Pressure
- **Support:** WTB (Weak Toward Bottom) → Bearish Pressure
- **Conflict:** One side pushing UP, other pushing DOWN (Away from center).
- **Result:** Market EXPANDS its range. Highly Volatile.
- **Range:** WTT-1 to WTB+1.

### Scenario 9: Bearish Support vs Bullish Resistance (The Squeeze)
- **Resistance:** WTB (Weak Toward Bottom) → Bearish Pressure
- **Support:** WTT (Weak Toward Top) → Bullish Pressure
- **Conflict:** One side pushing DOWN, other pushing UP (Toward center).
- **Result:** Market SQUEEZES/CONSOLIDATES.
- **Range:** EOR+1 to EOS-1.

**General Rule:** If you see Bullish pressure from one side and Bearish from the other, the market is in **Scenario 8 or 9 (Consolidation/Conflict)**. Tread carefully.
