# WTB (Weak Towards Bottom) on LTP Calculator

## Definition
WTB (Weak Towards Bottom) = Support or Resistance is trying to shift towards LOWER strike prices (bottom of option chain).
When WTB is present, a YELLOW BOX appears in the Volume or Open Interest column.

IMPORTANT — Option chain is INVERTED:
- Smaller strike prices are displayed at TOP of screen (bottom of option chain)
- Larger strike prices are displayed at BOTTOM of screen (top of option chain)
- "Bottom" = lower/smaller strike prices

---

## What Causes WTB?
The SECOND-HIGHEST value in Volume or OI column is at a LOWER strike price than the current support/resistance.
This means the market is considering shifting that level DOWNWARD.

Example:
- Support at 23000. Yellow box appears at 22,900.
- Support is WTB because it is trying to shift to LOWER strike price (22,900).

---

## WTB at Support — What it means:
- Support is trying to shift to a LOWER strike price
- BEARISH pressure — support about to break, market will fall further
- WTB % INCREASING = support shifting down = market going DOWN more (bearish)
- WTB % DECREASING = support becoming strong = market may bounce (bullish)
- WTB DISAPPEARS (% drops below 75%) = support becomes strong = BULLISH pressure (EOR+1 becomes target)

## WTB at Resistance — What it means:
- Resistance is trying to shift to a LOWER strike price
- BEARISH pressure — resistance moving down toward support, confirming downward move
- WTB % INCREASING = resistance shifting down = market going DOWN (bearish, COA 1.2 or 1.4)
- WTB % DECREASING = resistance becoming strong = bullish
- WTB DISAPPEARS = resistance becomes strong = BULLISH pressure (EOR+1 becomes market top)

---

## Percentage Rules (Game of Percentage — WTB)

### WTB at Support (example: Support at 24400, WTB at 24300 = 92.93%):
- Scenario 1: WTB % INCREASING (92.93% → 95% → 99% → turns PINK/highest)
  → support shifts from 24400 to 24300 → BEARISH (market falls to 24300)
  → After shift: yellow box appears at 24400 — this is NATURAL WEAKNESS, IGNORE IT

- Scenario 2: WTB % DECREASING (92.93% → 90% → 85% → 80%)
  → highest becoming stronger relative to second highest → support at 24400 becoming strong
  → BULLISH (market will reverse at EOS of 24400)

- Scenario 3: WTB % drops to 75% → yellow box DISAPPEARS
  → Support is now STRONG → BULLISH pressure
  → Market may break resistance and reverse from EOR+1

### WTB at Resistance (example: Resistance at 24500, WTB at 24400 = 83.29%):
- Scenario 1: WTB % INCREASING (83.29% → 90% → 99% → turns highest)
  → resistance shifts from 24500 to 24400 → BEARISH (market reverses at 24400 now, not 24500)
  → After shift: yellow box at 24500 — NATURAL WEAKNESS, IGNORE IT

- Scenario 2: WTB % DECREASING (83.29% → 80% → 78%)
  → resistance at 24500 becoming strong → BULLISH (resistance moving lower probability decreasing)
  → Market reverses from 24500 as before

- Scenario 3: WTB % drops to 75% → yellow box DISAPPEARS
  → Resistance becomes STRONG → BULLISH pressure
  → Increases probability resistance breaks out → market reverses from EOR+1

---

## Natural Weakness After Shifting (CRITICAL — IGNORE THIS)
After resistance shifts from 24500 to 24400:
- 24500 automatically shows yellow box (second highest now)
- This yellow box at 24500 looks like WTT — but it is NOT
- This is NATURAL WEAKNESS from the shift — IGNORE IT
- Only consider 24500 yellow box as real WTT if:
  1. Yellow box at 24500 first disappeared completely
  2. Then yellow box reappeared at 24500 again

## WTB Conditions (based on whether S/R is Volume-only, OI-only, or both)
(Same logic as WTT — see Condition 1, 2, 3 in wtt.txt — applies symmetrically for WTB direction)
- Condition 1: Single factor determines S/R → S/R status mirrors that factor
- Condition 2: Other factor creates WTB by moving toward imaginary line and crossing S/R → S/R becomes WTB (resistance) or WTT (support)
- Condition 3: Both weak, one WTB one WTT → priority to factor moving CLOSER to imaginary line
