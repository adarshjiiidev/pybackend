# AI LTP Calculator & Advanced Definitions

## AI LTP Predictions

The AI module of LTP Calculator provides real-time scenario analysis.
- **Scenario Identification:** Automatically detects if market is in Scenario 1, 2, etc.
- **Risky Levels:** Identifies "Risky Top" and "Risky Bottom".
- **Visuals:** Plots EOR, EOS, and breakdown levels on the chart.

## Max Pain & Max Gain

- **Max Pain:** The strike price where Option Writers (Sellers) will lose the least money. Market tends to expire near here.
- **Max Gain:** Theoretical target for option buyers (less reliable).

## Reversal Math (Details)

- **EOR (Extension of Resistance):** Strike Price + Delta/Gamma calculation.
- **EOS (Extension of Support):** Strike Price - Delta/Gamma calculation.
- **+1 / -1:** Refers to the NEXT strike price deviations.
  - If Resistance is 17500. EOR is near 17500.
  - EOR+1 is related to 17550 (Next strike).
  - Used for Averaging or Stoploss placement.

## Glossary of Terms

- **Imaginary Line:** Current ATM division.
- **Shift:** Boss changing house (Highest OI moving).
- **Consolidation:** COA 2.0 both stable.
- **Blood Bath:** Free fall (Scenario 6).
- **Bull Run:** Sky rocket (Scenario 7).
- **Sheesh Aasan:** Upside down market view (Bottom strikes at legs, Top strikes at head).
